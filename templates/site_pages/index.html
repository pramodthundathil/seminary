A Message from the Founder

"In 2002, while serving as a missionary in India, I met many sincere believers who longed to study God's Word but were unable to attend a Bible college because of financial limitations..."

By God's grace, what began as a small ministry has grown into an international theological seminary, equipping pastors, missionaries, church leaders, and believers from around the world through affordable, fully online, and biblically faithful education.
